Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Fair Split: Rent Calculator ("the App") is developed by FunApps. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The App works entirely on your device. Room details, rent amounts, and calculation results are stored locally on your phone and are never transmitted to any server.

Data Storage

All data you enter into the App (room names, sizes, features, and rent amounts) is stored only in your device's local memory during your session. We do not use databases, cloud storage, or any form of remote data persistence.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Sharing Feature

The App includes a "Share" feature that uses your device's native sharing functionality. When you share results, the content is sent directly through the app you choose (e.g., Messages, WhatsApp). We do not intercept, store, or have access to shared content.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages and collects no data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
