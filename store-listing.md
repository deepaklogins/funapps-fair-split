## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Tired of arguing about who should pay what? Fair Split calculates the perfect rent split based on what each room actually offers.

🏠 HOW IT WORKS
Simply add each room in your apartment, rate its features, and Fair Split instantly calculates how much each person should pay.

📐 SMART SCORING
Each room is scored based on real factors that affect value:
• Room size (square footage)
• Private bathroom access
• Natural light & windows  
• Closet space
• Balcony or patio
• Noise level

💰 FAIR FOR EVERYONE
The person with the biggest, brightest room with an en-suite bathroom pays more. The person in the smaller, darker room pays less. Simple, fair, and transparent.

📤 SHARE RESULTS
One tap to share the breakdown with your roommates via text, WhatsApp, or any messaging app.

✨ PERFECT FOR
• New roommates moving in together
• Friends splitting an apartment
• Couples with unequal bedrooms
• Anyone who wants a fair deal

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
No account needed. No data collected. Everything stays on your phone.

No ads. No subscriptions. Just fair rent.
